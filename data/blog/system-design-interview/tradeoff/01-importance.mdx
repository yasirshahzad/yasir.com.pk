# 🔷 1. What are Trade-offs?

### ✅ Key Idea

* Every design decision = **gain something, lose something**
* No perfect solution ❌

---

### 🔥 FAANG Question

**Q:** What are trade-offs in system design?
**A:**
Choosing between competing factors like **consistency, availability, performance, and cost**

---

### 🧠 Script

"Every system design decision involves trade-offs between competing factors."

---

# 🔷 10. Interview Gold (How to Speak)

### ✅ Always Say This Pattern

👉 "We have two options:

* Option A → pros, cons
* Option B → pros, cons
  Given requirements, I choose X because…"

---

### 🔥 FAANG Question

**Q:** How should you present trade-offs in an interview?
**A:**
Compare options and justify your choice based on requirements

### 🧠 Script
